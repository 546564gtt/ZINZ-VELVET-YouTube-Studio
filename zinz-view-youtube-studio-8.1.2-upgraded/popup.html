Enable video effectsIndependent of the Velvet appearance

Grade the picture in real time. Global controls affect the whole frame; Targeted Color Lab offers 74 spectrum targets plus dedicated Skin Tones and Browns & Tans controls. Turning on Effects automatically enables the renderer and a subtle Natural detail baseline; Auto Color is optional.

Effects use WebGL2 for the full multiscale pipeline, WebGL1 for selective color plus fine/mid-band detail, and Canvas 2D for the same core color and spatial-detail controls at adaptive lower resolution. Auto Color is not required. Advanced temporal denoising, guided filtering and edge-aware upscaling remain WebGL2-only. DRM-protected video can block pixel access, in which case selective and texture processing cannot be applied safely.

Material DepthIndependent multiscale detail enhancement

Advanced denoising and edge-aware upscaling remain WebGL2-only.
